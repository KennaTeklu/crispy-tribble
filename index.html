How it works

Brain takes the documents you already have and turns them into a spatial map you can navigate, so structure stops living inside a wall of text.

1
Upload a file — PDF, DOCX, TXT, EPUB, slides or spreadsheets. The file is opened and read on your device; it is never uploaded to a server.
2
Brain extracts the structure — headings, topics and relationships become nodes in a 3D graph you can rotate, zoom and wander through.
3
Study and quiz yourself — generate questions from your own content (or write them yourself), then answer, self-grade and fill in the blanks.
4
Watch mastery spread — every node tracks your understanding and changes color from red to green, so weak areas glow before the exam does.

About

Brain is built and operated by try4ever — an independent, one-person software project based in Arizona, USA. I made it because I needed a better way to organize my own studies, and outlines kept hiding the structure I was trying to learn.

The product has one guiding rule: your study material belongs to you. Files are processed on your device, stored in your browser, and never uploaded anywhere. There is no account database holding your notes and no analytics following you around. The only data that ever leaves your device is an email you choose to send, and a payment handled by Stripe if you subscribe.

Questions, bugs, or access issues — write to support@try4ever.com. I answer within 5 business days, usually faster.

No – works for anything: USMLE, MCAT, history, programming, whatever. Any material you can put in a PDF, DOCX, TXT or EPUB file.

Everything is stored in your browser. No cloud, no tracking. Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded. Clearing your browser storage deletes your data – it belongs to you.

Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026

The short version: your study material never leaves your device. Brain was built so that there is nothing to leak.

What stays on your device.

Your uploaded files, your knowledge maps, your quizzes and your progress are processed and stored locally in your own browser (IndexedDB). We have no cloud copy, no account database and no access to them. Clearing your browser storage deletes them permanently.

What can leave your device, only by your action.

An email or DM you send us (we use it to answer you, then it's just conversation history). If you subscribe, your payment details go to Stripe – we never see or store card numbers.

What we don't do.

No analytics, no ad networks, no trackers, no cookies set by us, no selling data – there is no data to sell. This landing page is fully self-contained: styles, scripts and icons are embedded in it, and it makes no external requests. The study app additionally loads its document-processing libraries from public CDNs when you use it; those CDNs see standard request data (IP, user agent) but never your files.

Your control.

Delete your data any time by clearing browser storage for this site, or remove a single map inside the app. Questions or deletion requests: support@try4ever.com, answered within 5 business days.
