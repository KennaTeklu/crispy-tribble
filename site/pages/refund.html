Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective September 20, 2026

The summary: you can always cancel in one click, and you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. The free plan – a short application – lets you use the real product before any payment, which is why paid charges are final.

Refund policy – all sales final.

Once a paid period starts, payments are non-refundable, including for partial use, unused time, or forgotten cancellations. There are exactly two exceptions, both billing errors on our side: (1) you were charged twice for the same period; (2) you were charged an amount you did not select. Email support@try4ever.com with the Stripe receipt and we will correct it.

How to cancel.

In the app: Settings → Manage Subscription → Cancel Subscription. That's one click, anytime, no retention maze. You can also cancel by email to support@try4ever.com. Cancelling stops future charges immediately; your access continues until the end of the current paid period.

Before you pay.

The free plan requires no card: if you have not entered card details with Stripe, nothing can be billed to you. Use it to learn the whole product first – when you do pay, it should be because the map is already earning its keep.

Questions.

support@try4ever.com – we respond within 5 business days.
