Features

Everything runs in your browser. No install, no account database, no cloud copy of your material.

🌌3D layout

See how everything connects. Rotate, zoom, wander around your knowledge. Distance and clusters mirror how topics relate.

🎨Color-coded mastery

Red means "I don't know", green means "got it". It's a progress bar in space — weak areas are impossible to ignore.

📝Quizzes & blanks

Generate questions from your content and test yourself. No AI required — you can use any tool or just write them yourself.

🔒Private by design

Your files are processed on your device and stored in your browser. No cloud, no account database, no tracking.

📴Works offline

Once loaded, Brain keeps working without a connection. Study on a plane, in a basement, in a hospital hallway.

📂Works with your files

PDF, DOCX, TXT and EPUB in. Scanned PDFs are OCR-read on your device (first 10 pages). Slides and spreadsheets extract too.
