Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026

The short version: your study material never leaves your device. Brain was built so that there is nothing to leak.

What stays on your device.

Your uploaded files, your knowledge maps, your quizzes and your progress are processed and stored locally in your own browser (IndexedDB). We have no cloud copy, no account database and no access to them. Clearing your browser storage deletes them permanently.

What can leave your device, only by your action.

An email or DM you send us (we use it to answer you, then it's just conversation history). If you subscribe, your payment details go to Stripe – we never see or store card numbers.

What we don't do.

No analytics, no ad networks, no trackers, no cookies set by us, no selling data – there is no data to sell. This landing page is fully self-contained: styles, scripts and icons are embedded in it, and it makes no external requests. The study app additionally loads its document-processing libraries from public CDNs when you use it; those CDNs see standard request data (IP, user agent) but never your files.

Your control.

Delete your data any time by clearing browser storage for this site, or remove a single map inside the app. Questions or deletion requests: support@try4ever.com, answered within 5 business days.

Changes.

Material changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.
