FAQ

Is this for a specific exam?

No – works for anything: USMLE, MCAT, history, programming, whatever. Any material you can put in a PDF, DOCX, TXT or EPUB file.

Is my data safe?

Everything is stored in your browser. No cloud, no tracking. Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded. Clearing your browser storage deletes your data – it belongs to you.

When you want the full product, the paid plan is $19/month and activates instantly.

Who builds Brain?

try4ever – an independent solo software project in Arizona, USA. That means fast answers and no support-ticket maze: email support@try4ever.com and you reach the person who wrote the code.

How are payments handled?

Subscriptions are billed in USD through Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe's certified infrastructure – they are never seen or stored by Brain.

What file types work?

PDF, DOCX, TXT and EPUB. Scanned PDFs are OCR-processed on your device (first 10 pages). Images, slides and spreadsheets are extracted locally too.

I'm drowning in lecture slides. How fast can this help?

Immediately. Upload the PDF and Brain builds the map on your device in minutes – you see the whole course at a glance and start quizzing the same hour. Most people find their first weak topic before their coffee gets cold.

Studying for USMLE, MCAT or NCLEX – does it work for me?

Yes. Those exams reward exactly what Brain is built for: seeing connections, retrieving instead of rereading, and knowing what is still red before test day. Upload your class notes, review sheets or study guides and study the map.

I'm not a med student – law, business, engineering, languages?

Brain works on any material you can put in a PDF, DOCX, TXT or EPUB. Case law, tax code, thermodynamics, Japanese grammar – if you can study it, you can map it.

I'm a teacher. Is this another tool trying to replace me?

No – and we mean it. AI should sharpen a teacher, never replace one. You prepare the material; Brain turns it into maps, quizzes and color-coded mastery your whole class can see. It takes the busywork, you keep the craft.

I'm a parent. Can I actually see my child's progress?

Yes. The mastery colors – red to green – are easy to read at a glance, so you can support without hovering. Brain gives you a shared, honest picture instead of "I already studied, mom."

Do I need a credit card?

No. The free plan needs no card at all, and the application is a short email. Card details only enter the picture – through Stripe – if you choose the $19/month plan.

What happens after I apply?

You start studying immediately. There's no waiting room: your application is reviewed while you already use your map – 21 nodes to start, more unlocked as it is approved.

Can I use it offline and on any device?

It runs in your browser – laptop, desktop, tablet. Once loaded, it works offline: study on the bus, in the library basement, on a plane. Your data lives in your browser, not in a cloud.

Why a 3D map instead of flashcards?

Flashcards drill isolated facts; a map shows how they connect – and exams mostly test connections. Brain does both: it generates the quizzes from your material, so you get retrieval practice inside the structure.
